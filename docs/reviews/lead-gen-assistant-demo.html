An AI helper for lead gen. Would it make your day easier?

The idea is simple. You connect Claude or ChatGPT to Magnus with your normal Magnus login. Then you can ask it about your day in plain words, and it can do some of the typing for you. Below are twelve short scenes of how that could look. Watch them, then tell us what you think.

1 · Watch
Each scene plays when you scroll to it. Use Play, Step and Restart under it to go at your own pace.
2 · React
Under each scene, tap one button and answer a question or two. Short answers are fine. So is "I wouldn't use this".
3 · Send
At the bottom, press Copy my feedback and paste it to whoever sent you this page. Your answers stay on your device until you do.

Before you startSix rules this helper would follow

1
It never messages anyone. It can write a draft. You send it from your own Instagram.
2
It asks before it saves. Captures and respark results are read back to you first. Nothing is saved until you say yes.
3
You can ask about your own numbers only. It can't tell you a teammate's numbers or tell a teammate yours. Admins already see everyone's numbers in Magnus, and their helper would too.
4
No phone numbers, emails or payments. Magnus doesn't give it those.
5
Every number comes from Magnus. It doesn't add things up or guess. If Magnus doesn't have a number, it says so.
6
Magnus stays the same. Everything you do today still works. This is an extra way to do it.

Part 1Your day

1Starting your shift

Sofia opens Claude and asks one question. She gets what the Capture and Respark pages would show her, in one answer.

2Checking people before you message

Today, the only way to find out if someone is already in Magnus is to capture them. That counts as a dupe, and you can't undo it. With the helper, Sofia could check first. Nothing is saved and nothing is counted.

3Saving a list from one reel

Sofia pastes a reel and eight handles from its comments. The helper tells her what it's about to save and what it's leaving out. She says yes once, and the list is saved.

4Working a respark batch

The helper gets Sofia's batch, tells her about a person, and drafts a first message. Sofia sends it herself. Later she says who she messaged, and the helper records it after reading it back.

5Your numbers at the end of the day

Sofia asks from her phone. The numbers are the same ones My Activity shows.

Part 3Ideas for later

These three don't exist anywhere yet, not even in Magnus. We're asking whether they're worth building.

7What happened to my people?Idea

Today you can't see what happened to the people you captured. This would show how far they got. It would never show money.

8Ending your shiftIdea

Sofia ends her shift with a sentence. Magnus counts her day and posts a short report to the team channel, with her note.

9Flagging a mistakeIdea

You can't undo a capture. Only an admin can remove one. This would let you flag a wrong capture so an admin sees it.

Part 4What your admin could ask it

Admins would get their own version. It shows them what the Operations page already shows them, plus a view of the current day. We'd like to know how these look from your side.

11Your admin checking the day
