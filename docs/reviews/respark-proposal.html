Respark: a second pass on everyone who stalled

Lead gen captures thousands of people a week, and most of them stall before they book. Respark hands each lead generator a batch of those people in a fixed order. For each one, they look at what the CRM knows and decide whether to try again or mark them not a fit.

  • 57,013people in the pool today
  • 4buckets, plus not a fit
  • 40–50per batch
  • 90days to inactive, a setting

The ask

What operations asked for

From a conversation with the operations lead on 23 October, translated from Spanish. One quote is yours.

  • In the CRM where they upload their leads, a list of 40–50 leads that weren't booked but were qualified, shown to each lead generator by time of day. For the night shift, the leads from Europe.Operations lead
  • Lead gen's job is volume. The opener's job is quality.You
  • Imagine doing that with 3,000 leads a week. I need them focused on the conversations more than filling in data.Operations lead, on openers marking no fit
  • We give them the list of 40. Thirty are good: they mark them resparked, and in the backend they count as an entry. Ten are bad: they mark them no fit, and those don't count as an entry.Operations lead
  • In theory, in conversation is automatic until a resparker marks it no fit.Operations lead
  • Closers have a third of the conversations openers handle. Lead generators don't leave notes either: they have 66% more volume than openers.Operations lead
  • Notes stay, but don't set the stageOpeners keep writing notes. Whether a note exists no longer decides if someone is in conversation; the capture does. Lead generators aren't asked for notes, and the Slack form idea is dropped.
  • In conversation from the first touchA capture puts the person in conversation, whether they're new or already known.
  • The lead generator decides not a fitDuring a respark, after looking at the chat, not during the first conversation.
  • A respark counts as an entryToward the lead generator's volume, like a capture. Not a fit doesn't count.
  • Batches by shiftThe night shift gets people in Europe. The CRM doesn't know where most people are yet (decision 3).
  • Not a fit isn't the endThose people stay in the pool, last in line, because they may be a fit later.

The model

Three states, each in its own place

"In conversation", "not a fit" and "inactive" sound like one status, but they answer three different questions. Kept apart, a person who had a call months ago can come back into conversation, and the CRM still knows they once showed.

Where the three states show up

Lifecycle change

A capture means in conversation

Lead gen messages the person when it captures them, so the first touch is already a conversation. A capture, new person or not, moves them to In conversation if they're behind it, and counts as a touch either way.

The pool

Who's in the pool, in priority order

The pool is a query, not a list anyone maintains. It's worked out when a lead generator asks for a batch, from each person's stage, disposition, deals and last touch. Each person counts once, in the first bucket they match.

The respark screen

One batch, one card per person

It lives in the lead gen portal, beside capture. A lead generator asks for a batch and gets the top 40–50 people from the pool, held for them so no one else gets the same people. Each card has what's needed to decide in a few seconds, and the profile is one click away.

What it writes

What each action records

Every action is one transaction and one touch on the person's timeline, credited to the lead generator. Nothing is deleted, and no deal moves.

Reports

Where resparks are counted

A respark counts as an entry, like a capture, so it shows up where lead generators and operations already look at volume. Not a fit is counted too, but never as an entry.

Before a plan

Seven decisions

Each has a recommendation. Answer the ones you disagree with, and the rest go into the plan as written.

1Bucket order, and order inside a bucket

  • Order: qualified-never-booked, never qualified, inactive, had a call and lost, not a fit
  • Inside a bucket: most recently stalled first, or oldest first

RecommendedThe order above, most recently stalled first: a person who stopped answering last month is warmer than one from April.

2Credit when a resparked person books

  • The original capturer keeps lead gen credit
  • The resparker gets it instead
  • Both: a second lead gen credit row with source "respark"

RecommendedBoth. Credit rows are only ever added, and a respark source keeps the two apart in reports.

3Batches by shift and region

  • Skip region for now
  • Lead gen records a country at capture
  • Route only people whose time zone is known (from a booking)

RecommendedSkip it in the first version. Most people are only a handle; a country field at capture is a separate, small change if operations wants it.

4Batch size and how long it's held

  • 40, 50, or a setting
  • Held until the end of the shift, or for a fixed number of hours

RecommendedA tenant setting, default 45, held until the business day ends. Unworked people go back to the pool.

5Undoing not a fit

  • Reopens on its own if the person qualifies, books or is resparked
  • Only an admin reopens it

RecommendedReopens on its own, the way unresponsive already does. A booking from a not-a-fit person shouldn't be hidden.

6The existing "unresponsive"

  • Keep it: an expired Slack deal sets it (4,291 people)
  • Fold it into inactive

RecommendedKeep it. It says something else, that they were qualified and went quiet. It doesn't change their bucket.

7The Prospect stage

  • Keep it, unused
  • Retire it: touches the leads report, the stage counts and the Prospects view

RecommendedKeep it unused for now and retire it later, once reports read In conversation as the entry stage.

Shipping

How it would ship

This is a feature, outside the migration phases. Each step can deploy on its own.

  1. DocsThe plan and the ADR amendment. Capture enters In conversation; not a fit can come from a lead generator; inactive is a rule; respark is a touch and a lead gen credit source.
  2. LifecycleCapture enters In conversation, and the backfill of the 81,091 people at Prospect, with its assert.
  3. SettingInactive after N days on the tenant settings page, and the inactive badge on the person page.
  4. BackendThe pool: the bucket query and its indexes, batches, the held-for mark, and the three actions with their touches, credit and disposition.
  5. FrontendThe respark screen in the lead gen portal: the batch, the read-only person panel and its permission, and the clearer capture confirmations.
  6. ReportsEntries and not a fit in the lead gen reports, per worker, with resparks counted beside captures.
